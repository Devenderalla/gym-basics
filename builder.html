How the builder thinks

Big compound movements first while you're fresh, smaller accessory work after, core near the end, and conditioning only when your goal calls for it. Longer durations add exercises rather than dragging out rests. Whatever it generates, the weight rule never changes: the lightest weight that lets you finish every rep with control.
