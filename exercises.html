Reading the prescriptions

3 × 8–12 · rest 90s means three sets of eight-to-twelve repetitions with ninety seconds of rest between them. The level filter re-shows each card with the prescription for that level. Weight is always the same rule: the lightest that lets you finish every rep with control.
