The whole thing in six lines

Protein is the one that matters — 1.6 g per kilo of bodyweight as a beginner, rising toward 2.2 as you advance and higher again in a deficit, every day, training or not. Calories decide the direction — more than you burn and you gain, fewer and you lose, and the training decides what kind of weight moves. Carbohydrate is the training fuel, so it rises on the days you lift and falls on the days you don't. Fat has a floor you should not go under, around 0.6 g per kilo. None of this needs a supplement — dal, curd, eggs, paneer, chana and chicken cover it. And the scale is the referee: if your weight is not moving the way the plan says it should after a fortnight, the plan is wrong, not the scale.
